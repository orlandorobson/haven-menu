Executive decision

The proposed prices remain competitive

Most of the Head Barista's proposed changes place Haven near the middle of the Salalah specialty-cafe market and below premium Muscat and GCC price anchors.

San Sebastian at OMR 1.800 would still be one of the strongest-value offers in its direct Salalah comparison set.

Principal reservation

Brownie at OMR 2.500 is premium for Salalah. The serving must visibly justify it through portion, chocolate quality, ice cream, sauce or presentation.

Important caveat

July is within Khareef. Current sales are affected by tourism, holiday behaviour and greater customer traffic, so volume alone cannot prove price sensitivity.

Dessert economics

San Sebastian is profitable, but not at a high food margin

The OMR 7 difference between cake cost and sales at the current price is gross contribution, not net profit. Labour, service materials, waste, occupancy, payment costs and tax still have to be covered.

7Gross contribution
53.3%Direct food cost

Proposed price · OMR 1.800

18Revenue per cake
10Gross contribution
44.4%Direct food cost
At OMR 1.800, Haven improves contribution by OMR 3 per whole cake while remaining at the bottom of the observed Salalah San Sebastian range.

Why the low price may still be valuable

  • It encourages an easy add-on to coffee.
  • High turnover reduces spoilage.
  • It gives Haven a memorable value signature.
  • It may generate repeat visits and recommendations.

Why high sales do not prove causation

  • San Sebastian is already a fashionable category.
  • Texture and coffee pairing are naturally attractive.
  • Staff recommendation and visibility matter.
  • Khareef increases traffic and holiday spending.
Decision: move to OMR 1.800, then measure slices per 100 transactions, coffee attachment and total contribution. A small fall in units can still be a successful result.

Brownie requires stronger justification

Local brownies with ice cream can appear around OMR 1.600-1.800. OMR 2.500 is defensible only as a generous premium serving. If the product is a simple slice, retain OMR 2.300.

Questions before publication

Iced Cappuccino It remains OMR 1.500 while Iced Latte becomes OMR 1.600. If cup size and ingredient cost are similar, price both at OMR 1.600.
Hot Hazelnut Latte It remains OMR 1.500 while Caramel is OMR 1.700 and Iced Hazelnut becomes OMR 1.700. Consider OMR 1.700 standard / 1.900 large.
Affogato At OMR 1.400 it becomes unusually inexpensive beside the revised desserts. Consider OMR 1.600-1.700 if premium vanilla ice cream and full espresso are used.

Product proof

At OMR 1.700, Mojitos should genuinely show fruit, mint and citrus rather than reading as soda and syrup. At OMR 2.500, the Brownie description must say what makes the serving premium. Pricing and language should prove one another.

Implementation and evidence

Approve carefully, then measure

Recommended decision

  1. Approve all proposed coffee and Iced Tea changes.
  2. Approve Mojitos at OMR 1.700 subject to ingredient quality.
  3. Approve San Sebastian and Chocolate Cheesecake at OMR 1.800.
  4. Approve Brownie at OMR 2.500 only with visible product proof; otherwise retain OMR 2.300.
  5. Resolve Iced Cappuccino, hot Hazelnut Latte and Affogato before publishing.

Four-week measurement

  • Units sold and revenue
  • Direct product cost and waste
  • Gross contribution
  • Percentage sold with coffee
  • Average ticket containing the item
  • Customer comments related to price
For San Sebastian, compare slices per 100 transactions, coffees attached per 10 slices, and contribution per 100 transactions. Do not compare raw July volume with a quiet-season week.
