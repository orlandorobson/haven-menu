# Haven Cafe Pricing Study

Date: 26 July 2026  
Market: Osara, Salalah, Dhofar  
Decision status: Research only. No menu prices changed.

## Executive Conclusion

The Head Barista's proposed drink prices remain competitive. Most would place Haven near the middle of the current Salalah specialty-cafe market and below premium Muscat and GCC anchors.

The dessert picture is less uniform:

- San Sebastian at OMR 1.800 would still be conspicuously good value in Salalah.
- Chocolate Cheesecake at OMR 1.800 remains competitive and coherent with San Sebastian.
- Brownie at OMR 2.500 would sit toward the premium end of the local market. It is justified only if the portion, chocolate quality, plating, or accompaniment visibly supports it.

Recommended action:

1. Approve all proposed drink changes.
2. Approve both cheesecake changes to OMR 1.800.
3. Hold Brownie at OMR 2.300 unless it is served as a visibly premium plated dessert; otherwise approve OMR 2.500 and update its description to state exactly what is included.
4. Consider moving Affogato from OMR 1.400 to OMR 1.600 or 1.700 if Haven uses premium vanilla ice cream and a full espresso shot.
5. Review Iced Cappuccino and hot Hazelnut Latte for consistency before publication.

## Method

The comparison uses currently indexed consumer menus, direct cafe menus, and dated photographs of physical counter menus. Talabat figures are now labelled as delivery-channel evidence rather than assumed in-store prices. Platform pricing can include commission recovery, channel-specific pricing, promotions, or a later menu revision.

The strongest same-cafe check is 2 Oz: its physical menu photograph is dated July 2026 and its Talabat menu was crawled in July 2026. Rovana also has an identifiable physical menu photograph, but it dates from August 2025, so differences there may reflect both channel pricing and menu changes over time.

Portion sizes, sauces, and serving formats may still differ from Haven's offer. Promotional prices and elaborate plated desserts were not treated as exact equivalents.

## San Sebastian Economics

Given:

- Whole-cake purchase cost: OMR 8.000
- Portions: 10
- Direct cake cost per portion: OMR 0.800

At OMR 1.500:

- Revenue per whole cake: OMR 15.000
- Gross profit before other costs: OMR 7.000
- Direct food cost: 53.3%
- Gross margin: 46.7%

At OMR 1.800:

- Revenue per whole cake: OMR 18.000
- Gross profit before other costs: OMR 10.000
- Direct food cost: 44.4%
- Gross margin: 55.6%

The OMR 7.000 is contribution before staff time, sauce or garnish, plates and washing, napkins, card charges, spoilage, rent, utilities, delivery commission, and VAT treatment. It is positive, but a 53.3% direct food cost is high relative to common cafe pastry targets around 25-30%.

The cake may nevertheless be strategically valuable at a lower margin if it:

- brings customers into Haven;
- increases coffee attachment;
- raises the total ticket;
- turns first-time visitors into repeat guests;
- has very low spoilage because volume is high.

Therefore, the best metric is contribution per customer visit, not margin percentage on the cake alone.

## Why San Sebastian May Be Selling

Price is likely one factor, but not the only plausible explanation:

1. San Sebastian is already a popular and recognisable cafe dessert.
2. Its creamy texture pairs naturally with bitter espresso.
3. OMR 1.500 removes hesitation and encourages an add-on.
4. The portion may feel generous relative to the price.
5. It may be more consistent or visually appealing than nearby alternatives.
6. Staff may be recommending it.
7. The improved menu and photography may be making it easier to notice.
8. July falls within Dhofar's Khareef tourism season, when customer volume and holiday spending rise.

No single week of high sales can distinguish these causes.

Keep the established grammatical forms:

- Iced Spanish Latte
- Iced Latte
- Iced Hazelnut Latte
- Iced Tea
- Iced Dark Mocha
- Iced White Mocha

Use "Iced," not "Ice," in the published English menu.

The iced-coffee ladder becomes understandable:

- Americano on Ice: OMR 1.300
- Iced Cappuccino: OMR 1.500
- Iced Latte: OMR 1.600
- Iced Caramel Latte: OMR 1.700
- Iced Hazelnut Latte: OMR 1.700

- Iced Dark/White Mocha: OMR 1.800

Two points need review:

1. Iced Cappuccino remains OMR 1.500 while Iced Latte moves to OMR 1.600. This is defensible only if its portion or milk quantity is smaller; otherwise price both at OMR 1.600.
2. Hot Hazelnut Latte remains OMR 1.500 while Caramel Latte is OMR 1.700 and Iced Hazelnut becomes OMR 1.700. If syrup quantity and cost are comparable, move hot Hazelnut Latte to OMR 1.700, with large at OMR 1.900.

### Dessert ladder

At the proposed prices:

- Affogato: OMR 1.400
- San Sebastian: OMR 1.800
- Chocolate Cheesecake: OMR 1.800
- Brownie: OMR 2.500
- Truffle Mango: OMR 2.500

Affogato becomes unusually inexpensive for a dessert containing both premium ice cream and espresso. OMR 1.600-1.700 would still be accessible and would make the ladder more coherent.

Brownie at OMR 2.500 needs product proof. If it includes vanilla ice cream, sauce, premium chocolate, or a generous warm portion, say so in English and Arabic. If it is a simple slice, retain OMR 2.300.

## Measurement Plan

Because the change occurs during Khareef, evaluate it by product mix rather than raw sales alone. Record weekly for at least four weeks:

- units sold;
- revenue;
- direct product cost;
- waste;
- gross contribution;
- percentage sold with a coffee;
- average ticket containing the item;
- customer comments or refusals linked to price.

For San Sebastian, compare:

- slices per 100 transactions;
- coffees attached per 10 slices;
- contribution per 100 transactions.

If units fall after moving to OMR 1.800 but total San Sebastian contribution and coffee attachment remain healthy, the increase has worked. If attachment collapses, consider restoring OMR 1.500 as a deliberate signature-value price rather than treating it as an error.

## Regulatory Presentation

Oman's standard VAT rate is 5% for most taxable goods and services. The Consumer Protection executive regulation requires cafes and restaurants to display a price list prominently, include the amount of tax where applicable, and use Arabic as one of the displayed languages. Confirm whether Haven's displayed OMR prices are VAT-inclusive and state this consistently on the web and print menus.
